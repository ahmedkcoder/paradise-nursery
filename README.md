# Paradise Nursery Shopping Application

An interactive e-commerce web application built with React and Redux Toolkit that allows users to browse houseplants, view detailed information, manage a shopping cart, and calculate total costs dynamically.

## Company Description
Welcome to Paradise Nursery, where we bring nature closer to your home. Our mission is to provide a wide variety of healthy, vibrant houseplants that purify the air and enhance your living spaces. Whether you are a seasoned plant enthusiast or just starting your green journey, we are here to help you cultivate your indoor oasis.

## Features
- Landing page with background styling and a "Get Started" entry point.
- Categorized product listing with at least 6 houseplants per category.
- Dynamic shopping cart with Redux state management.
- Add to cart functionality with button disabling and item quantity tracking.
- Interactive cart management (increment, decrement, remove, total calculation).
